I.

Now

At Embraceable Technology I sit between the boardroom and the codebase: advising leadership on where AI money should go, then owning the roadmap and the team that delivers it.

The technical centre of that work is Multi-Space Reasoning (MSR) — an architecture I invented that reaches best-in-class accuracy without fine-tuning, which cuts the cost and effort of every deployment. MSR was selected for “AI in the Alps”, presented alongside 40 leading AI scientists including Sepp Hochreiter. Alongside it, e1, our deductive-reasoning AI, won the IONOS Partner Award for AI Project of the Year 2025.

V.

Research roots

Before industry, nearly a decade of research across some of Europe’s strongest labs — the Max Planck Institutes for Psychiatry and for Biological Cybernetics, Leibniz-Institut für Wissensmedien, TU Chemnitz, GEOMAR and Fortiss — after starting as a Junior Research Fellow at the Indian Statistical Institute.

That work produced an IEEE VR 2015 Doctoral Consortium paper (Arles, France), an oral presentation at VCSS 2015 (Rostock) and an IEEE VR travel grant, and led to selection for the IMPRS-TP PhD track at the Max Planck Institute of Psychiatry. It is why the reasoning systems I build today are meant to be inspectable, not just accurate.

Today I pass some of it on as a Machine Learning Mentor at ReDI School, Munich.
